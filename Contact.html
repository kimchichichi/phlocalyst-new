Before you email

LOGISTICS FAQ · ANSWERS
TO THE TOP 8 QUESTIONS
What does a typical Phlocalyst live set involve?
A solo set runs 60–90 minutes — Roland SP-404 MKII, a laptop running Ableton, and live trumpet. For larger rooms we tour as a trio (Phlocalyst + drums + keys). Tech rider is available on request — write to booking.
How long does it take to clear a sync?
From first email to signed paperwork: usually 2–4 weeks for standard ad / TV use, faster for time-sensitive requests if you flag the deadline up top. Nettwerk holds master rights for the recorded catalogue; publishing is administered separately. The fastest sync we've cleared was 36 hours; the slowest, a Netflix series, took 11 weeks.
Do you do custom commissions / scoring?
Yes, selectively. About 4–6 per year. Typical projects: short film, podcast intro, brand identity sting, dance piece. Lead time is usually 6–8 weeks from brief to delivered stems. Write to hi@phlocalyst.com with the brief, deadline, and budget.
I'm a producer — can you record trumpet for my track?
Sometimes, with the caveat above: send a stem with space in it, not a finished track. Typical turnaround is 2 weeks once we've agreed on direction. Rates depend on the project — non-commercial / indie projects are often free or barter; major-label work is invoiced.
Do you reply to demos?
Personally and slowly — about 1 in 5 get a reply, and usually weeks late. Sending the same track twice doesn't speed it up. The best way to get listened to is to send one track with one sentence of context, not a whole album with a press kit.
Can I sample your music?
For commercial release: write to Nettwerk for a sample clearance — they handle it efficiently and we say yes more often than not. For non-commercial / SoundCloud / playlists: credit and a link are enough, no need to ask.
What's the best subject line to get a fast reply?
"[date] · [venue or context] · [what you need]". Example: "12 Jun · Botanique Brussels · 75min headline slot." That gets read first. Subject lines like "Big opportunity!" get read last.
I just want to say thanks for a record.
Please do — hi@phlocalyst.com. He reads all of these even if the reply takes a while. Thank-you mail is roughly half the reason the rest of this works.
