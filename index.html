Phlocalyst — Michiel De Vleeschhouwer
Lo-fi producer & classical trumpet player, based in Munich by way of Flanders.
Available for features & live sets
May 2026 — Munich, DE

Phlocalyst makes jazz-hop — muted trumpet looped over warm, dust-flecked beats and phased Rhodes. By day he plays in a professional orchestra; by night he sketches with an iPad, an SP-404 and a horn he's held since he was five. Two-hundred million streams later, the music still sounds like a room you used to know.
