Bio — 002

Day job:
Orchestra.
Night job:
Beats.

Phlocalyst is the recording name of Michiel De Vleeschhouwer — born in Sevilla, raised in Flanders, trumpet in hand since age five. He plays third trumpet in a professional Munich orchestra. The other half of the day, he's in headphones with an iPad and an SP-404, looping his own horn into the kind of dust-warm beats that quietly cross 200 million streams while nobody's watching.

He started in 2017 to release the stress of his classical career — Pete Rock and J Dilla in the morning, Mahler at night. Insights, his debut LP with Nettwerk, was conceived during a single quarantine in February.
