Static artist site for Phlocalyst (Michiel De Vleeschhouwer). No build system — JSX transpiled in-browser by Babel. Serve with `python3 -m http.server 8082`.

Key artist facts (CLAUDE.md is authoritative): Flemish, born Sevilla, raised Flanders. All pages say "Portugal-based" citing Spotify bio; a 2026 single called "Lisboa" corroborates Portugal connection. CLAUDE.md says Munich since 2016 — discrepancy unresolved, leave Portugal until confirmed.

**Why:** Data files (discography.md) were last hand-verified in 2023 but the live Music.html was updated with Apple Music/Spotify data through May 27 2026. Always check Music.html (not discography.md) for current release data.

**How to apply:** When asked about "recent releases," check Music.html — it has 19+ 2026 singles (latest: "Best Me" May 22, 2026) plus 2 EPs (Now And Then feat. Mr. Käfer; The Cool Hour w/ xander.) that the discography.md doesn't list.
