The story

Chapter 01
Jazz
+ Hip Hop

Phlocalyst is a Flemish producer based in Portugal, blending his classical training with a passion for Jazz and Hip Hop. Born in Sevilla and raised in Flanders, his music carries the warmth of southern Europe and the precision of a classical education.

Rooted in a family of musicians and shaped by years as a solo trumpet player in the orchestral world, he now channels his experience into crafting warm, soulful jazzy beats.

Phlocalyst began making music in 2017. By Nettwerk's December 16, 2022 release announcement for Insights, his catalogue had surpassed 200 million streams on Spotify.

On stage, Phlocalyst blurs the boundary between DJ and live performer — weaving his live trumpet into the set, improvising over beats in real time. Each show is part concert, part controlled experiment in jazz and electronics.

Now based in Portugal, his music reflects a life lived across borders: Flemish precision, Iberian warmth, and the unhurried soul of lo-fi hip hop — a sound that has found a quiet home in the record collections of more than 200 million listeners worldwide.
